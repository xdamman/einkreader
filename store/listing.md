## Full description (max 4000 chars)

```
An RSS reader made for your e-ink device.

einkreader turns an e-ink tablet into a calm reading device: your feeds,
newsletters, bookmarks and saved links in one quiet, high-contrast,
black-and-white app — no animations to smear, no infinite anything.

OFFLINE FIRST
Sync once, read anywhere. Articles download in full, images included.
Threads are stitched together whole. Your reading position is remembered
per article, and half-read stories wait for you under Resume reading.

A READING PIPELINE, NOT AN INBOX
Feed → To Read → Read → Highlights → Shared. Swipe right to mark
anything read. Filter by folder, source, or author. Universal search
covers every source, title, article body, and highlight you have saved.

HIGHLIGHTS & NOTES
Select any passage to highlight it instantly. Tap a highlight to add a
private note. Everything lands in one Highlights tab — and in a single
human-readable highlights.md file you own.

YOUR FILES, NOT OURS
The whole library is plain Markdown in a clear year/source folder
structure on your device. Back it up, sync it, open it in any editor —
in twenty years it will still be readable. No accounts, no analytics,
no lock-in.

SHARE ON YOUR TERMS
Local and private by default. If you want an audience, create a free
public profile: a name@einkreader.app address people can follow, and a
page at einkreader.app/you showing the highlights you chose to share.
Share a quote with your comment, copy a link to it, or send it by
email — you decide, every time.

SOURCES
• RSS and Atom feeds, organized in folders
• Twitter/X bookmarks (with your own free API key), whole threads included
• Nostr profiles: notes, long reads and bookmarks
• Any link, via Android share or paste — saved to read later
• Email anything to your @einkreader.app address (optional plugin)

MADE FOR E-INK
Designed on and for devices like the iFLYTEK AINOTE and Boox tablets:
pure black-and-white, generous type, discrete page refreshes, and
gestures that don't fight the screen. Works on regular phones and
tablets too.

einkreader.app — the RSS reader made for your e-ink device.
```

## Data safety questionnaire (draft answers)

- Data collected: none by default.
  - Optional features: email address (the whitelisted sender, if the
    user enables the Email plugin) — stored to provide the feature, not
    shared, deletable on request. User-created public content (username,
    shared highlights) — published at the user's explicit request.
- Data shared with third parties: none. (Content the user publishes to
  Nostr relays is user-initiated sharing, not data sale/transfer.)
- Encryption in transit: yes (HTTPS/WSS).
- Deletion: local data deleted with the app; server-side data on request.
- No ads, no analytics SDKs, no advertising ID.
