Changelog

What's new in each version (smaller updates in between are gathered into the next one). Many changes start as feedback; follow along with the changelog feed, in einkreader of course.

Version 0.6.0 · September 25, 2026 · Download

Public feedback and smoother X

  • Share ideas and bugs with everyone from the app, reply in threads and react with emoji.
  • A brand page with logos and images at einkreader.app/brand.
  • X bookmarks are fetched 10 at a time, and only as far back as needed.
  • Fixed signing in to X on e-ink devices getting stuck in a loop.
  • Only new bookmarks are fetched; the debug log is quieter.
  • Tapping a link opens its menu instantly, even offline.
  • Errors that need X reconnected offer a button to do it.
  • The Read tab is sorted by when you read each article.
  • Links to X posts show the post itself; more articles extract cleanly.
  • Resume reading sits below the filters and shows only what they select.
  • Problems with X are explained in plain sentences.
  • A source that fails to update shows a warning, and X can be reconnected from its feed.
  • Articles from table-based sites (like paulgraham.com) extract properly.
  • Paragraph breaks and list lines survive in quotes.
  • Sharing a highlight closes the dialog at once and publishes in the background.
Version 0.5.0 · August 16, 2026 · Download

Public pages and a calmer app

  • A button at the end of an article shares all your highlights from it.
  • Articles show whether they're downloaded; the Read and Highlights tabs filter by source.
  • A short tweet sharing a link brings in the linked story.
  • Clear messages when you're offline, everywhere; an Advanced section in Settings.
  • Stray style code no longer shows up as text in articles.
  • Tables in articles render properly.
  • Settings are organized into sections, one screen each.
  • Small fixes to how profiles look.
  • Hidden page content stays hidden; highlights work across bold text.
  • A clearer profile editor.
  • Pages without a declared encoding no longer show garbled accents; copy an article's link from the share menu.
  • The profile screen mirrors your public page, with an editable address.
  • Simpler public profile pages with a link for each story, and ways to subscribe.
Version 0.4.0 · August 5, 2026 · Download

Search, every X bookmark, several profiles

  • Use several profiles and switch between them, each with its own library.
  • Writing a note and sharing a highlight happen in one place.
  • Long tweets keep their paragraphs and lists.
  • Narrow search results by source, author and more.
  • Search across sources, authors, titles, content and highlights.
  • Filter X bookmarks by author.
  • Your whole X bookmark history is imported, resuming where it stopped.
Version 0.3.0 · July 24, 2026 · Download

A new home and your own email address

  • A redesigned home screen; swipe right on an item to read it.
  • Highlighting is instant, with a menu right where you tapped.
  • A free name@einkreader.app address, and a public page for your highlights.
  • Email articles to your reading feed.
  • X threads arrive whole, with their images.
  • Saved articles are organized by year and source.
  • Fixed the app failing to open after updating to 0.2.0 (no data was lost).
Version 0.2.0 · July 21, 2026 · Download

Nostr, folders and sharing

  • Follow Nostr profiles: their notes, long reads and bookmarks.
  • An optional public profile to share highlights with comments.
  • Anything that can't be sent right away waits in an outbox.
  • Newsletter calls to action are hidden in articles.
  • Highlights stay in sync with a highlights file in your library folder.
  • Folders expand right in the feed's filters.
  • Drag sources into folders; see each source's description and stats.
  • Post highlights to X, sized to fit your plan.
  • Organize sources into folders; a share menu for articles.
  • Save any link to read later, from other apps or the clipboard.
  • Save links found inside articles to read later.
  • The app remembers where you stopped reading.

First public beta
