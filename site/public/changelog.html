Changelog

What's new in each version. Many changes start as feedback; follow along with the changelog feed, in einkreader of course.

Version 0.6.13 · October 10, 2026 · Download

Forwarded newsletters stay readable

  • Substack newsletters forwarded or sent to your @einkreader.app address keep their whole text, with their title, byline and images, instead of being replaced by the publication's subscribe page.
  • Pictures that link to a larger version show as pictures, not as a stray "!".
Service update · October 10, 2026

Author profiles on the web

  • On the web feedback page, an author's name or avatar opens their profile.
Version 0.6.12 · October 10, 2026 · Download

Reading settings: font, margins and page turns

  • New Reading section in Settings, with a live preview: text size, margins, and four fonts (Serif, Literata, Sans-serif, Atkinson Hyperlegible).
  • Turn pages instead of scrolling: articles move a screen at a time, with "← Page 2 / 5 →" at the bottom. Swipes and page keys turn pages too.
  • The text size is now remembered.
