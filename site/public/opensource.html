Open source

einkreader's source code is public. The app is built in the open, and technically-minded readers can inspect it, build it themselves, or contribute:

github.com/xdamman/einkreader

What that means in practice

  • The full app source is on GitHub, including the reading engine, the sync pipeline and the sharing features.
  • Signed APKs of every release are published on the releases page — the self-built channel for people who prefer sideloading.
  • Your data never depends on us either way: everything the app stores is plain Markdown on your device.

What runs where?

Reading is free and fully local. The optional plugins (email-to-feed, Twitter cross-posting) run on our servers.
